Three weeks ago, a [draft](https://d2y7tnbi225lw.cloudfront.net/2025-09/Conceptversie-NGWI-2026-voor-raadpleging.pdf) of the new Netherlands Code of Conduct for Scientific Integrity was released for public comment. At META/e we gave it a thorough, line-by-line read. In many respects, the revision is a solid step forward, but we see five major issues that still need attention.

1. Responsibilities should go beyond researchers or research groups
2. Problematic characterization of the principle “Independent” in relation to academic freedom
3. The limited role of monitoring and reporting in institutions’ duty of care
4. Excessive burden on witnesses of integrity violations
5. Fraudsters can too easily escape accountability

You can read the complete feedback [here](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/metaes-reflections-new-netherlands-code-conduct-integrity-vaesen-vsjqe/).
