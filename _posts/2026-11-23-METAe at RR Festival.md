TU/e and META/e had a strong presence at the national [Recognition & Rewards Festival 2026](https://www.aanmelder.nl/recognitionandrewards/home), held on November 12 in Amersfoort to mark six years of the Dutch Recognition & Rewards programme. During the plenary morning programme, TU/e policy colleague Julma Braat took the stage for the *Show & Tell* session with her pitch "*Responsible Academic Assessment: From Principles to Practice*", sharing how TU/e translates policy principles into concrete institutional practice. Immediately following the panel, META/e members Daniël Lakens and Sajedeh Rasti delivered the morning keynote, exploring what it takes to make large-scale academic collaboration work and how to fairly recognize and reward everyone's contributions. Later in the afternoon inspiration market, Andrea Kis and Julma Braat showcased a mini version of *Behind Closed Doors: The Academic Assessment Game*, inviting attendees to experience their interactive role-playing game on academic evaluation.
